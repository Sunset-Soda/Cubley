## Cubely 1.0.0 🎲

The first release of Cubely: a Rubik's Cube scrambler, solver and timer in one app.

### What's inside
- **Scrambler** – random scrambles and patterns, with an animated 3D move guide, a flip view and a net view to check your cube against.
- **Solver** – colour in a flat net of your own cube and get a step-by-step solution on the guide cube. Mistakes are flagged live while you paint.
- **Timer** – press `Space` to start and stop. Solves are saved and graphed with averages of 5 and 12, plus DNF marking.
- **Ten themes**, each with its own colours, art, fonts and light and dark versions: Cherry Pop, Grape Grunge, Camp Glitter, Midnight Diary, Chrome Chaos, Scarlet Scribble, Crate Jungle, Lonely Shore, Noir Witch and Meadow Quest.
- **Phones and foldable cover screens:** a fixed, no-scroll layout with swipeable pages (Guide / Scramble / History, Colour in / Solution, Timer / Graph). The theme picker shows the full theme name and every control fits without scrolling, and the solver's how-to guide has its own page. Landscape phones and narrow screens are covered too. Unfolded screens get two columns that fit without overlap or scrolling.
- Themed drop-downs for Pattern, Moves and Speed that match each theme and scroll smoothly.
- Fills the whole window when maximised, and works fully offline (fonts are bundled).

### First-run notes
The apps aren't code-signed, so your system may warn you the first time.
- **Windows:** SmartScreen → *More info* → *Run anyway*.
- **macOS:** right-click the app → *Open*.
- **Android:** open the .apk and allow installing from this source. It adapts to foldables: two columns on the unfolded inner screen, and it resizes as you fold and unfold.
- **Linux:** `chmod +x Cubely-1.0.0.AppImage`, then run it. Some distros need `libfuse2`.

Your times, painted cube and theme are saved locally and kept when you update.
