## Cubely 1.1.0 🎲

Six new themes, a light/dark switch, and a Learn tab.

### What's new
- **Six new themes** (sixteen in total), each with its own colours, background art, header icon, fonts and light and dark versions: **Static Spark**, **Lucha Libre**, **Ember Wing**, **Crescent Sparkle**, **Midnight Drift** and **Ringside Rush**. They use colours and general motifs only, with no characters or logos.
- **Auto / Light / Dark switch** under the theme picker. Auto follows your system; your choice is remembered.
- **Learn tab** with a beginner track that follows the official Rubik's solve guide, and an F2L track. The Solver can also solve with the beginner method and with F2L.
- Fixed the Static Spark header icon being the wrong size.

### First-run notes
The apps aren't code-signed, so your system may warn you the first time.
- **Windows:** SmartScreen → *More info* → *Run anyway*.
- **macOS:** right-click the app → *Open*.
- **Android:** open the .apk and allow installing from this source. It adapts to foldables: two columns on the unfolded inner screen, and it resizes as you fold and unfold.
- **Linux:** `chmod +x Cubely-1.1.0.AppImage`, then run it. Some distros need `libfuse2`.

Your times, painted cube and theme are saved locally and kept when you update.
