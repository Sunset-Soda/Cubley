        How to copy your cube onto the net
        • Hold the cube with white on top and green facing you, then read each face while looking straight at it.
        • The net unfolds like a box: top above front, then left, front, right and back across the middle, bottom below front.
        • Top face: the row nearest the front is the bottom row of its square. Bottom face: the row nearest the front is the top row of its square.
        • To see the back face, turn the whole cube around sideways so white stays on top. Do not flip it over.
        • Colours on one corner or edge piece are always different, and never a pair of opposites (white and yellow, green and blue, red and orange).
        • A very common slip is copying the sides with the cube tilted. Re-check any square marked in red against your cube first.

                How to copy your cube

                Previous scrambles

                  Scrambles you move past are kept here, up to eight. Tap one to bring it back.

                  U D L R F B are the six faces. A move on its own is a clockwise quarter turn as you look at that face, ' is counterclockwise, and 2 is a half turn. No face repeats back to back, and no axis gets three moves in a row.
